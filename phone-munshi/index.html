A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before the first call.

Can I keep my business number?

You can forward calls from your existing number to the assistant number. Forwarding support and charges depend on your mobile carrier.

Does it read my phone’s call history?

No. The Android app does not request call-log access. Its call history shows calls answered by the assistant.

How can I try Phone Munshi?

Contact us for a demo and current availability. View the pricing above. In-app purchases are coming soon.

How are call minutes counted?

Each call is rounded up to the next whole minute using the calling service’s reported total duration. Voicemail, forwarding or fallback portions can count. See the billing terms.

Can prices change later?

Prices may change for future purchases. An increase to an existing subscription renewal requires at least 30 days’ advance notice, or longer where required, and any required consent. Paid periods and minutes already purchased are protected. See the terms.

Where can I read the policies?

Read our privacy policy, terms and refund policy. For account or data deletion, email support.
