These Terms govern access to and use of Phone Munshi, an AI receptionist service for tradesmen operated by Sonkawade Labs.

Service description

Phone Munshi answers calls that a tradesman cannot take. Calls forwarded from his number are answered by the Phone Munshi AI agent, which speaks to the caller, works out what job is needed, proposes a time and a price, and sends the job to the tradesman for confirmation. It can also follow up with the caller on WhatsApp and send a payment link for a confirmed job.

These Terms apply to the tradesman who subscribes to Phone Munshi.   • You must only forward a number that you are entitled to use.
