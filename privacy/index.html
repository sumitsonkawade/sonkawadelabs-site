English

1. Who We Are

CSC Pro is a Windows desktop SaaS product operated by Sonkawade Labs for Common Service Centre (CSC) operators in Maharashtra, India. The software helps operators prepare, review, manage, and automate government application workflows, including submissions on the Aaple Sarkar portal.

In many cases, the CSC operator decides what applicant information is entered into the system. Depending on the context, Sonkawade Labs may act as a service provider, processor, or data fiduciary for limited purposes described in this Policy.

2. What Data We Collect

We may collect or process the following categories of data:

  • Applicant and customer identity data, including name, mobile number, address, date of birth, gender, and related application details.
  • Government ID and document data, including Aadhaar-related data. Where Aadhaar numbers are displayed, stored, or exported by CSC Pro, they are intended to be masked where reasonably possible, except where full document images are uploaded by the operator for lawful processing.
  • Application workflow data, including form selections, eligibility details, uploaded records, supporting documents, operator notes, extracted document details, submission status, and audit logs.
  • WhatsApp-related data, including phone number, WhatsApp message content, media attachments, message status events, templates, delivery metadata, and customer response history when WhatsApp workflows are enabled.
  • Operator account and device data, including email, login events, device-binding identifiers, license status, and security logs.
  • Billing and transaction references related to software subscriptions, credits, or payments. We do not state that we store full card or banking credentials.
Primary personal data Name, phone number, application details, and supporting documents.
Sensitive context data Aadhaar-linked records, caste, income, residence, and related certificate inputs.
WhatsApp data Messages, attachments, delivery callbacks, and linked phone numbers.

3. How We Collect Data

  • Directly from CSC operators using CSC Pro.
  • From applicants or customers through documents, forms, or WhatsApp messages shared with an operator.
  • From uploads, scans, document processing, support requests, device security checks, and system logs.
  • From service providers that support cloud storage, hosting, messaging, or application security.

4. How We Use Data

  • To prepare, validate, manage, and submit government application workflows.
  • To extract information from uploaded documents through automated processing.
  • To send service notifications to operators and their customers, including WhatsApp messages where enabled.
  • To maintain account security, software licensing, device binding, fraud prevention, diagnostics, support, and service continuity.
  • To maintain records required for billing, reconciliation, grievance handling, and legal compliance.

5. WhatsApp Business Platform Disclosure

CSC Pro uses the WhatsApp Business Platform as a Tech Provider integration to help CSC operators communicate with applicants and customers. When WhatsApp features are enabled, CSC Pro may process phone numbers, message content, document images, delivery status updates, template usage, and conversation history strictly for service communication and workflow support.

  • WhatsApp data is used to receive customer documents, send application-status notifications, respond to service queries, and support operator workflows.
  • We do not sell WhatsApp user data or message data to third parties.
  • WhatsApp data is processed subject to Meta platform rules, approved templates where applicable, and lawful use by the operator.

6. Storage, Security, and Location of Processing

CSC Pro stores data in encrypted local SQLite storage on the operator's Windows device and may also store or sync data in Supabase PostgreSQL and related cloud infrastructure used for service operation.

  • We use reasonable safeguards such as access controls, encryption at rest where configured, environment-based credentials, device binding, and audit logging.
  • Some processing may occur through third-party infrastructure such as Supabase, Railway-hosted services, Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, and related support providers.
  • No method of storage or transmission is completely risk free. Operators remain responsible for securing their devices, credentials, and exported files.
  • The sonkawadelabs.in website itself does not set cookies and does not run analytics or advertising trackers. Web fonts are loaded from a third-party CDN, which may receive your IP address as part of a normal web request.

7. Third-Party Sharing Policy

We may share personal data only where reasonably necessary with:

  • Government portals and authorities involved in the requested application workflow.
  • Service providers such as Supabase, hosting providers, Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, storage providers, security providers, and support vendors acting for service delivery.
  • Regulators, courts, law enforcement, or government bodies where disclosure is required by law or lawful order.
  • A lawful successor in the event of a merger, acquisition, or business transfer, subject to applicable safeguards.

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, account administration, operator support, security, audit, reconciliation, dispute handling, and legal compliance.

  • Application and customer records may be retained while the operator account remains active and for a reasonable period thereafter unless deletion is required sooner.
  • WhatsApp messages, attachments, and delivery metadata may be retained for workflow continuity, review, support, and audit needs.
  • Security logs, billing references, and compliance records may be retained as needed to protect lawful interests and satisfy legal obligations.

9. Data Deletion Instructions

Meta App Review requires a clear deletion process. A user, customer, or operator may request deletion of personal data associated with CSC Pro or WhatsApp communications by emailing sumitsonkawade@sonkawadelabs.in with the subject line "CSC Pro Data Deletion Request".

Please include, where available: full name, mobile number, CSC operator name, application reference, and the WhatsApp number used for communication.

  • We may first verify identity and request scope before acting on deletion requests.
  • Where Sonkawade Labs controls the relevant cloud-stored data, we will delete or anonymize it within a reasonable period unless retention is legally required.
  • Where data is stored locally on an operator's device, the operator may need to delete local records or exported files directly, and we may guide that process where feasible.
  • If a request relates to WhatsApp data, we may also need to retain limited logs necessary to demonstrate compliance, prevent misuse, or handle disputes.

10. User Rights

Subject to applicable law, users and data principals may request:

  • Access to a summary of personal data processed about them.
  • Correction or updating of inaccurate personal data.
  • Deletion or erasure of personal data, subject to lawful retention exceptions.
  • Withdrawal of consent for future processing where consent is the basis.
  • Information about the categories of third parties with whom data is shared.
  • Grievance redressal for privacy concerns.

11. Children's Data

CSC Pro is intended for adult CSC operators. If personal data relating to a minor appears in a government application workflow, the operator is responsible for ensuring lawful authority and appropriate consent or authorization from a parent, guardian, or other competent person.

12. Legal Basis and Indian Law

We process data based on user instructions, operator-provided consent or authorization, service delivery requirements, legitimate uses permitted by applicable Indian law, and compliance obligations. This Policy is intended to be read in line with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and applicable contractual or platform obligations.

Roles under the DPDP Act. For applicant and customer personal data that a CSC operator collects and enters into CSC Pro, the operator determines the purpose of processing and acts as the data fiduciary; Sonkawade Labs processes that data on the operator's instructions as a data processor. For operator account, licensing, billing, and security data, Sonkawade Labs acts as the data fiduciary. Each party is responsible for its own compliance obligations in the role it performs.

  • A data principal may withdraw consent at any time, with ease comparable to the manner in which it was given — by replying "STOP" on WhatsApp or emailing sumitsonkawade@sonkawadelabs.in. मराठी

1. आम्ही कोण आहोत

CSC Pro हे Sonkawade Labs द्वारे चालवले जाणारे Windows desktop SaaS सॉफ्टवेअर आहे. हे महाराष्ट्रातील Common Service Centre (CSC) ऑपरेटरसाठी बनवलेले असून Aaple Sarkar पोर्टलवरील शासकीय अर्ज तयार करणे, तपासणे, व्यवस्थापित करणे आणि ऑटोमेशनद्वारे सादर करणे यासाठी वापरले जाते.

बहुतांश प्रकरणांत कोणता अर्जदार डेटा सिस्टिममध्ये टाकायचा हे CSC ऑपरेटर ठरवतो. परिस्थितीनुसार Sonkawade Labs सेवा पुरवठादार, प्रोसेसर किंवा मर्यादित उद्देशांसाठी डेटा फिड्युशरी म्हणून काम करू शकते.

2. आम्ही कोणता डेटा गोळा करतो

  • अर्जदार किंवा ग्राहकाची माहिती, जसे नाव, मोबाईल नंबर, पत्ता, जन्मतारीख, लिंग आणि अर्जाशी संबंधित तपशील.
  • शासकीय ओळखपत्रे आणि दस्तऐवज, ज्यात आधार-संबंधित माहितीचा समावेश असू शकतो. शक्य तितक्या प्रमाणात आधार क्रमांक masked स्वरूपात ठेवण्याचा उद्देश आहे, परंतु कायदेशीर प्रक्रियेसाठी ऑपरेटरने अपलोड केलेल्या पूर्ण दस्तऐवज प्रतिमांमध्ये संपूर्ण तपशील असू शकतो.
  • अर्ज प्रक्रिया डेटा, जसे फॉर्म निवड, पात्रतेची माहिती, अपलोड केलेले कागदपत्रे, कागदपत्रांतून काढलेली माहिती, सबमिशन स्थिती आणि ऑडिट लॉग.
  • WhatsApp संबंधित डेटा, जसे फोन नंबर, संदेश मजकूर, मीडिया फाइल्स, डिलिव्हरी स्टेटस, टेम्पलेट वापर आणि संभाषण इतिहास.
  • ऑपरेटर खाते आणि डिव्हाइस डेटा, जसे ईमेल, लॉगिन नोंदी, डिव्हाइस-बाइंडिंग आयडेंटिफायर्स, लायसन्स स्थिती आणि सुरक्षा लॉग.
  • सदस्यता, क्रेडिट्स किंवा पेमेंटशी संबंधित व्यवहार संदर्भ. पूर्ण कार्ड किंवा बँकिंग क्रेडेन्शियल्स साठवले जातात असे आम्ही सांगत नाही.

3. डेटा आम्हाला कुठून मिळतो

  • CSC Pro वापरणाऱ्या CSC ऑपरेटरकडून थेट.
  • अर्जदार किंवा ग्राहकांनी ऑपरेटरला दिलेल्या कागदपत्रे, फॉर्म किंवा WhatsApp संदेशांतून.
  • स्कॅन, अपलोड, कागदपत्र प्रक्रिया, सपोर्ट विनंत्या, डिव्हाइस सुरक्षा तपासणी आणि सिस्टम लॉगमधून.
  • क्लाउड स्टोरेज, होस्टिंग, मेसेजिंग किंवा सुरक्षा सेवा देणाऱ्या तृतीय पक्ष पुरवठादारांकडून.

4. आम्ही डेटा कसा वापरतो

  • शासकीय अर्ज तयार करणे, पडताळणे, व्यवस्थापित करणे आणि सादर करणे.
  • अपलोड कागदपत्रांतून स्वयंचलित प्रक्रियेच्या मदतीने माहिती काढणे.
  • ऑपरेटर आणि ग्राहकांना सेवा-संबंधित सूचना पाठवणे, ज्यात सक्षम केल्यास WhatsApp संदेशांचा समावेश आहे.
  • खाते सुरक्षा, सॉफ्टवेअर लायसन्सिंग, डिव्हाइस बाइंडिंग, फसवणूक प्रतिबंध, तांत्रिक निदान आणि सपोर्ट देणे.
  • बिलिंग, ताळमेळ, तक्रार निवारण आणि कायदेशीर पालनासाठी नोंदी ठेवणे.

  • WhatsApp डेटा ग्राहकांकडून कागदपत्रे घेण्यासाठी, अर्ज स्थिती कळवण्यासाठी, सेवा प्रश्नांची उत्तरे देण्यासाठी आणि ऑपरेटर वर्कफ्लो समर्थित करण्यासाठी वापरला जातो.
