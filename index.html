CSC Pro Live

Desktop software for Common Service Centre operators. The operator photographs a customer's Aadhaar, the software reads it and fills the MahaOnline certificate application in Chrome by itself. 15 government services, plus a PMFBY crop-insurance module. ₹10 per completed application from a prepaid wallet.

Download for Windows · Privacy · Terms

Arzi Live

A citizen describes a problem on WhatsApp in any Indian language. Arzi asks the two or three questions it still needs, then sends back a ready-to-print application letter addressed to the right office, as a PDF. ₹10 per application, no subscription.

Start on WhatsApp · Privacy · Terms

Helpora Support Live

A multi-tenant AI support assistant that sits on a client's own website as one script tag. It answers from the client's own knowledge base, can only link to pages they approved, escalates to their team, and publishes the same knowledge as crawlable help pages. There is a free plan, and paid plans start at ₹999 a month.

helpora.sonkawadelabs.in · Privacy · Terms

Wearly AI Live

Describe a T-shirt in your own words and an AI designs three of them. You buy the one you like and it is printed to order and couriered to you. Design generation is paid for with credits at ₹1 per credit; the garment itself is priced per product with shipping included, and we deliver within India only.

wearlyai.sonkawadelabs.in · Privacy · Terms

How it works

Every product replaces the same thing — someone stopping their work to answer, type or file. Pick a product to see its flow.

1

Describe the problem

A citizen sends a WhatsApp message describing the issue or request, in the Indian language they are most comfortable using.

2

The details get structured

Arzi interprets the message, extracts the essential details, and prepares a formal application letter addressed to the right authority.

3

Ready to print

The finished PDF is sent back to the same WhatsApp chat, ready to print or submit. ₹10 per application, pay per use.

1

Open the customer record

The operator launches CSC Pro, creates or selects the customer, and uploads their documents for the certificate being applied for.

2

Documents prefill the form

Aadhaar and supporting documents are read automatically, prefilling the application fields so the operator only reviews and corrects.

3

Automated submission

CSC Pro runs the MahaOnline workflow and submits. One credit charge of ₹10 covers the whole chain — nothing is billed separately.

1

Add it to your site

Helpora appears as a chat button on your existing website. No redesign, and no change to how your site is built.

2

Give it your content

You add your FAQs, help articles and useful links from a dashboard. Helpora answers from that, and only points customers to links you approved.

3

It answers, or hands over

Common questions are answered day or night. When one needs a person, the conversation passes to your team with what the customer already said.

1

Describe the shirt

You type what you want printed — a phrase, a joke, a picture in your head. Generating the designs spends credits, bought at ₹1 per credit.

2

Pick from three designs

An AI returns three variants. You choose one, place it on the garment and size you want, and see the finished price before you pay.

3

Printed to order

Nothing is printed until payment is confirmed. Printing takes 2–3 working days and delivery 3–7 more, anywhere in India, with shipping already in the price.

1

Connect the shop

A small Windows agent reads the shop's live stock and prices straight out of TallyPrime — the accounting software the shop already uses.

2

Customers ask, any way they like

They message the shop's number in their own language, or they ring it. A call nobody answers forwards to the agent, which picks up and asks what they need.

3

The owner stays informed

Answers match what is actually on the shelf, and the owner gets a summary of what customers asked for — including items worth restocking.

1

The call is missed

You are on site with your hands full and the call goes unanswered. Phone Munshi notices it on your own Android phone.

2

The agent answers it

It WhatsApps the caller in Marathi, Hindi, Hinglish or English — or takes it by voice — works out the job, and suggests a time and a price.

3

You confirm the job

The details reach you on WhatsApp. One tap confirms or declines — nothing is booked until you say so, and the money goes to your own UPI ID.

Pricing

Priced per job, not per seat. All amounts are in Indian Rupees (INR) and payable in India only. These are the current published rates referred to in each product's Terms of Service.

Arzi · CSC Pro

₹10 per application
  • Arzi: pay per use — no credit packs, no subscription, no minimum purchase
  • CSC Pro: paid from a prepaid wallet, ₹1 = 1 credit, minimum top-up ₹100, no fixed packs and no bulk discount
  • Credits are deducted only when an application is actually submitted — a failed or abandoned application costs nothing
  • Credits do not expire while your account is active

Helpora Support

₹999 per month and up
  • Free ₹0 (250 AI replies a month, 1 seat, no card required, not time-limited) · Starter ₹999 (1,000 replies, 2 seats, 60 voice minutes) · Growth ₹2,499 (5,000 replies, 5 seats, 150 voice minutes) · Scale ₹5,999 (20,000 replies, unlimited seats, 250 voice minutes)
  • All plans are billed per month; annual costs ten months' price for twelve
  • Prepaid — nothing renews automatically, no card or UPI mandate is stored
  • Beyond a plan: ₹0.60 per extra AI reply · full plan details

Wearly AI

₹1 per credit
  • Credits pay for AI design generation. Packs of 50, 100 and 300 credits, at ₹1 = 1 credit with no bulk discount
  • Credits are spent only when a generation actually runs
  • The garment is priced per product and per size, shown in full before you pay — shipping within India is already included
  • Printed to order after payment is confirmed · delivery and refund terms

StockSaathi and Phone Munshi are not on sale yet — no payment is collected for them until their prices are published here. Payments are handled online in INR by Cashfree Payments (UPI, cards, net banking, wallets); card and bank details are entered on Cashfree's secure checkout and never stored by Sonkawade Labs. Prices shown are the final amount payable — Sonkawade Labs is not currently registered under GST, so no tax is added. Credits, plan access and documents are delivered electronically, normally within minutes and in any case within 24 hours. Wearly AI printed garments are physical goods, made to order after payment and delivered within India only — allow 2–3 working days to print and 3–7 more to deliver. Government and portal fees are separate and are not paid to Sonkawade Labs. The full published rates, tax position and delivery terms are on the Pricing page; when a payment can be refunded is in the Refund & Cancellation Policy.
