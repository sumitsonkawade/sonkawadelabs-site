How it works

From a message to a filed application

Both products collapse the same slow, repetitive chain into a few minutes of work. Pick a product to see its flow.

1

Describe the problem

A citizen sends a WhatsApp message describing the issue or request, in the Indian language they are most comfortable using.

2

The details get structured

Arzi interprets the message, extracts the essential details, and prepares a formal application letter addressed to the right authority.

3

Ready to print

The finished PDF is sent back to the same WhatsApp chat, ready to print or submit. ₹10 per application, pay per use.

1

Open the customer record

The operator launches CSC Pro, creates or selects the customer, and uploads their documents for the certificate being applied for.

2

OCR prefills the form

Aadhaar and supporting documents are read automatically, prefilling the application fields so the operator only reviews and corrects.

3

Automated submission

CSC Pro runs the MahaOnline workflow and submits. One credit charge of ₹10 covers the whole chain — nothing is billed separately.
