How it works

From a message to a filed application

Both products collapse the same slow, repetitive chain into a few minutes of work.

1

Describe the problem

A citizen writes to Arzi on WhatsApp in their own language, or an operator opens a new case in CSC Pro.

2

The details get structured

The request is turned into the right format — a formal letter for Arzi, or an OCR-checked application form for CSC Pro.

3

Ready to submit

Arzi sends back a print-ready PDF. CSC Pro submits to the government portal and deducts a single credit charge.
