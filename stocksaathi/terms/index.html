These Terms govern access to and use of StockSaathi, a stock-enquiry assistant for shops operated by Sonkawade Labs.

Service description

StockSaathi tells a shop's customers whether an item is available and what it costs, using the item and price data synced from the shop's own software, and reports enquiries back to the shop owner. Customers can ask on WhatsApp, or on a call forwarded from the shop's number when nobody picks up, which the StockSaathi AI agent answers by voice.

Call forwarding

  • Your telecom operator may charge for forwarded calls. Those charges are between you and your operator and are not paid to or collected by Sonkawade Labs.
  • Forwarding depends on your operator's network. We do not guarantee that every unanswered call will be forwarded or answered — calls can fail for reasons outside our control, including network outages and operator restrictions.
  • You must only forward a number that you are entitled to use.

Consent to recording

Calls answered by the AI agent are recorded and transcribed. The assistant identifies itself as automated and informs the caller at the start of the call. By enabling call forwarding to StockSaathi you authorise this recording on your behalf and confirm you are entitled to do so for calls made to your number.

Automated conversation

The agent speaks automatically and may mishear or misunderstand which item a caller is asking for, particularly on a poor line. Sonkawade Labs does not warrant that any transcript or answer given on a call is accurate.
