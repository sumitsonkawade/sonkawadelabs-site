Arzi Data Deletion Instructions

Product: Arzi

This page explains how users may request deletion of data related to Arzi. It can also be used as the public data deletion reference URL for Arzi-related Meta review flows.

Information that may be deleted

  • WhatsApp conversation data retained by the service, where available.
  • Generated application letter content associated with the request.
  • User identifiers and support records related to the deletion request, where legally permitted.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to sumitsonkawade@sonkawadelabs.in.
  2. Use the subject line Arzi Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include the phone number used with Arzi and any details that help identify the relevant conversation.

Response timeline

We will review the request and respond within a reasonable period, subject to technical availability, legal obligations, and abuse-prevention requirements.

Contact

For deletion requests or questions, contact sumitsonkawade@sonkawadelabs.in.
