About Us

Sonkawade Labs is a sole proprietorship owned by Sumit Sonkawade, based in Walandi, Deoni Taluka, Latur District, Maharashtra. We build software for citizen service workflows in India: Arzi, a WhatsApp assistant that turns a citizen's message into a ready-to-print application letter, and CSC Pro, desktop software that helps Common Service Centre operators collect customer details, read documents, and complete Maharashtra government certificate applications. Both are digital services sold online in India at ₹10 per application — see Pricing.
