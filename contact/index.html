About us

Who we are

Sonkawade Labs is a sole proprietorship owned by Sumit Sonkawade, based in Walandi, Deoni Taluka, Latur District, Maharashtra. We build software that handles the paperwork and the customer enquiries small Indian businesses and citizens would otherwise deal with by hand. All of it is sold as a digital service online in India, in Indian Rupees.

What we sell

  • Arzi — a WhatsApp assistant that turns a citizen's message into a ready-to-print application letter. ₹10 per application.
  • CSC Pro — desktop software that helps Common Service Centre operators collect customer details, read documents, and complete Maharashtra government certificate applications. ₹10 per application, from prepaid credits.
  • Helpora Support — an AI support assistant a business adds to its own website to answer customer questions. Monthly and annual plans from ₹999 per month, with a 14-day free trial.
  • StockSaathi — answers a shop's stock and price enquiries on WhatsApp and on calls the shop could not pick up. Pricing not yet published.
  • Phone Munshi — an AI receptionist for tradesmen that answers forwarded calls and passes the job over for confirmation. Pricing not yet published.

Arzi and CSC Pro are on sale today at ₹10 per application — see Pricing. StockSaathi and Phone Munshi are not available for purchase yet and nothing is charged for them. Payments are collected through our payment partner Cashfree Payments.
