✳︎ Phone Munshi

Never lose a customer to a missed call

Phone Munshi is a receptionist for plumbers, electricians, mechanics and other tradesmen. A call you can't take is forwarded to the AI agent, which answers it, finds out what work the caller needs, and passes the job to you to confirm.

What the product does

Picks up when you can't

Set your phone to forward the calls you don't answer. The AI agent answers them, speaks to the caller in their own language, asks what work they need and when, and keeps them from calling the next person.

For the tradesman

One tap to confirm

You get the job details on WhatsApp — what the work is, when, and for how much — and confirm or decline with a single tap. Confirmed jobs are recorded for you.

1

The call forwards

You are on site with your hands full and the call goes unanswered. Instead of ringing out, it forwards to your Phone Munshi agent.

2

The agent answers it

The agent picks up, says it is answering for you, talks to the caller in Marathi, Hindi or English, works out the job, and suggests a time and a price. It can follow up on WhatsApp too.

3

You confirm the job

You approve it with one tap, the customer is told, and the booking is saved. You can see how much work was booked this way each month.

Good to know

How call forwarding works

  • Only the calls you don't answer are forwarded. Calls you pick up yourself never reach the agent.
  • The agent tells every caller, up front, that it is an automated assistant answering on your behalf.
  • Calls answered by the agent are recorded and written down, so you can check what was agreed. See the Privacy Policy.
  • No job is booked until you confirm it. A time or price the agent mentions is based on your own rates.
  • Your telecom operator may charge for forwarded calls. Those charges are theirs, not ours.
