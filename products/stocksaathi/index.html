What the product does

Answers "is it available?"

A customer asks for an item — by WhatsApp message, or on a call the shop couldn't pick up. StockSaathi answers whether it is available and what it costs, without the owner stopping work.

For the shop owner

Keeps stock in sync

Stock and prices stay in step with the shop's existing accounting software, so the answers customers get match what is actually on the shelf.

1

Connect the shop

StockSaathi is set up with the shop's item list and prices from the software the shop already uses.

2

Customers ask, any way they like

They message the shop's number in their own language, or they ring it. A call nobody answers forwards to the AI agent, which picks up and asks what they need.

3

The owner stays informed

The owner gets a summary of what customers asked for — including items that were out of stock and worth restocking.

Good to know

How call forwarding works

  • Only the calls the shop doesn't answer are forwarded. Calls you pick up yourself never reach the agent.
  • The agent tells every caller, up front, that it is an automated assistant answering for the shop.
  • Calls answered by the agent are recorded and written down. See the Privacy Policy.
  • What the agent quotes comes from your own records — it is information, not a reservation of stock.
  • Your telecom operator may charge for forwarded calls. Those charges are theirs, not ours.
