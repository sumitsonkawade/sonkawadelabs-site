✳︎ Helpora Support

AI customer support for your website

Helpora Support adds a chat assistant to your website that answers customer questions from your own help content, and hands the conversation to your team when a person is needed.

What the product does

Answers your customers

A chat window on your website answers common customer questions in plain language, day or night, using the help content you have given it.

For your team

Escalates when needed

When a question needs a human, the conversation is passed to your team along with what the customer has already said, so nothing is repeated.

1

Add it to your site

Helpora appears as a chat button on your existing website. No redesign and no change to how your site is built.

2

Give it your content

You add your FAQs, help articles and useful links from a dashboard. Helpora answers from that, and only points customers to links you approved.

3

Watch the conversations

Your dashboard shows every conversation, what customers asked most, and where your help content has gaps.
