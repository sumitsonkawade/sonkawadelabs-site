What the product does

Core workflow

Helps operators collect customer details, process Aadhaar and related documents, and automate repetitive application steps on MahaOnline.

Supported use cases

Certificate workflows including caste, income, and residence applications for Maharashtra service operators.

Typical operator flow

Five steps

  • Launch Chrome and log into the required operator systems.
  • Open CSC Pro and create or select the customer record.
  • Upload customer documents and let the app prefill relevant fields.
  • Choose the certificate type and run the automated workflow.
  • Review the result and complete final submission steps.
Who uses it

Primary audience

CSC operators and center staff handling multiple citizen applications in a desktop environment.
