Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: July 7, 2026

This policy explains when payments made to Sonkawade Labs for CSC Pro subscriptions or prepaid credits can be refunded or cancelled. It should be read together with the Terms of Service and the Pricing page, which lists the current rates.

1. Prepaid Credits

Current rate: ₹10 per application for both CSC Pro and Arzi. CSC Pro is paid from prepaid wallet credits, minimum top-up ₹100. Prices are final and no additional tax is added — see the Pricing page.

  • Used credits are non-refundable. Once a credit is consumed for an application workflow, it cannot be reversed, restored, or redeemed for cash.
  • No refund is available merely because a government portal is slow, unavailable, rejects an application, or requires correction.
  • If a credit is deducted due to a verified fault in CSC Pro itself (for example, a duplicate deduction for the same application), we will re-credit the affected amount to your account after review.

2. Duplicate or Failed Payments

  • If you are charged more than once for the same top-up or subscription, the duplicate amount will be refunded to the original payment method after verification.
  • If a payment is debited but credits or subscription access are not delivered, and the payment gateway confirms the debit, we will either deliver the purchased credits or refund the amount.
  • Verified refunds are initiated within 7 business days of confirmation. The time for the amount to reflect in your account depends on your bank or payment provider (typically 5–10 business days).

3. Subscription Cancellation

  • You may stop using the service and cancel future renewals at any time by contacting us at the email below.
  • Cancellation stops future billing. Fees already paid for the current billing period are non-refundable except where required by law.
  • Unused prepaid credits remain available for use until account closure. On account closure, unused credits are forfeited unless a refund is required by applicable law or agreed in writing.

4. How to Request a Refund

Email sumitsonkawade@sonkawadelabs.in with the subject line "Refund Request", including: your registered email or mobile number, the payment reference / UTR / transaction ID, the payment date and amount, and a brief description of the issue.

We acknowledge refund requests within 48 hours and resolve them within 30 days.

5. Delivery

CSC Pro and Arzi are digital services. There is no physical shipping. Credits and subscription access are delivered electronically to your account, normally within minutes of payment confirmation and in any case within 24 hours.
