1. CSC Pro — prepaid credits

CSC Pro is billed from a prepaid credit wallet. 1 application = ₹10, end to end — that single charge covers the WhatsApp intake conversation with the citizen, document handling, the operator review step, and the automated submission to the government portal. Nothing in that chain is billed separately.

You top up the wallet with any amount you choose — ₹1 buys 1 credit and an application costs 10 credits, so every top-up works out to the same flat ₹10 per application. There are no fixed packs, no bonus tiers, and no bulk discount. The minimum top-up is ₹100.

  • Credits are deducted only when an application is actually submitted to the portal. A failed or abandoned application does not consume a credit.
  • Credits do not expire while your account is active.
  • Government fees, portal charges, and any service fee you choose to collect from your own customer are separate and are not paid to Sonkawade Labs.

Nothing renews automatically and no card or UPI mandate is stored — when a paid period ends, the workspace returns to trial limits unless another period is bought. Annual is charged at ten months' price for twelve months of service. Every account starts with a 14-day free trial and no card is required.

4. Taxes

Prices shown on this page are the final amount payable. No additional tax is added at the time of payment. Sonkawade Labs is not currently registered under GST, so GST is not charged or collected and a GST tax invoice is not issued. If GST registration becomes applicable in future, this page will be updated before any revised amount is charged.

5. Payment and delivery

Payments are accepted online in INR through our payment partner Cashfree Payments, which supports UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and wallets. Card and bank details are entered on Cashfree's secure checkout and are never stored by Sonkawade Labs. CSC Pro and Arzi are digital services with no physical shipping — credits and generated documents are delivered electronically, normally within minutes of payment confirmation and in any case within 24 hours. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy for refunds, duplicate payments, and cancellation.

6. Changes to pricing

Sonkawade Labs may revise these prices, change the credit rate or minimum top-up, or introduce new plans. Revised prices are published on this page and apply only to purchases made after the change. Credits already bought are honoured at the rate you paid. This page is the current published rate referred to in the Terms of Service.
