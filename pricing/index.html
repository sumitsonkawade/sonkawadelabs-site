Both products are priced the same way and there are no other charges. You pay per completed application — there is no setup fee, no monthly minimum, and no per-user licence fee. All amounts are in Indian Rupees (INR) and are payable in India only.

1. CSC Pro — prepaid credits

CSC Pro is billed from a prepaid credit wallet. 1 application = ₹10, end to end — that single charge covers the WhatsApp intake conversation with the citizen, document handling and OCR, the operator review step, and the automated submission to the government portal. Nothing in that chain is billed separately.

You top up the wallet with any amount you choose — ₹1 buys 1 credit and an application costs 10 credits, so every top-up works out to the same flat ₹10 per application. There are no fixed packs, no bonus tiers, and no bulk discount. The minimum top-up is ₹100.

3. Taxes

Prices shown on this page are the final amount payable. No additional tax is added at the time of payment. Sonkawade Labs is not currently registered under GST, so GST is not charged or collected and a GST tax invoice is not issued. If GST registration becomes applicable in future, this page will be updated before any revised amount is charged.

4. Payment and delivery

Payments are accepted online in INR. CSC Pro and Arzi are digital services with no physical shipping — credits and generated documents are delivered electronically, normally within minutes of payment confirmation and in any case within 24 hours. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy for refunds, duplicate payments, and cancellation.

Credits already bought are honoured at the rate you paid. This page is the current published rate referred to in the Terms of Service.
