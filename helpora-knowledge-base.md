## 2. Arzi — WhatsApp application-letter assistant

**Q: What is Arzi?**
Arzi is a WhatsApp bot. A citizen sends a message on WhatsApp describing a problem, complaint or
request in the Indian language they are comfortable with, and Arzi sends back a ready-to-print formal
application letter as a PDF, addressed to the relevant office or authority.

**Q: How does Arzi work, from the user's side?**
1. The user sends a WhatsApp message describing the issue or request.
2. Arzi interprets the message and picks out the essential details.
3. It prepares a formal application letter.
4. The finished PDF comes back to the same WhatsApp chat, ready to print or submit.

**Q: What does Arzi cost?**
₹10 per application, pay per use. No credit packs, no subscription, no minimum purchase. You pay only
for each application letter that is generated and delivered.

**Q: Who is Arzi for?**
Citizens, customers or support users who need help drafting a formal written application and would
rather describe the problem in their own language on WhatsApp.

**Q: Does Arzi guarantee my application will be accepted?**
No. Arzi helps with drafting only. Sonkawade Labs does not guarantee acceptance, approval, legal
sufficiency, or any action by an office, authority or institution. Review the letter before you
submit it anywhere.

## 3. CSC Pro — certificate workflow software

**Q: What is CSC Pro?**
CSC Pro is Windows desktop software for Common Service Centre operators in Maharashtra. It helps
operators manage customer intake, handle documents, communicate over WhatsApp, and complete the
repetitive steps of certificate applications on MahaOnline.

**Q: Which certificates does it handle?**
Caste, income and residence certificate workflows, and related Maharashtra government service
requests.

**Q: What does CSC Pro cost?**
₹10 per application, end to end, paid from a prepaid credit wallet. That one charge covers the
WhatsApp intake conversation with the citizen, document handling, the operator review step, and the
automated submission to the government portal. Nothing in that chain is billed separately.

**Q: How do the credits work?**
₹1 buys 1 credit, and an application costs 10 credits. The minimum top-up is ₹100. There are no fixed
packs, no bonus tiers and no bulk discount — every top-up works out to the same flat ₹10 per
application. You may top up any amount of ₹100 or more.

**Q: Do credits expire?**
No, credits do not expire while your account is active.

**Q: What if an application fails?**
Credits are deducted only when an application is actually submitted to the portal. A failed or
abandoned application does not consume a credit.

**Q: Are government fees included?**
No. Government fees, portal charges, and any service fee you choose to collect from your own customer
are separate and are not paid to Sonkawade Labs.

**Q: Where do I download CSC Pro?**
From the download link on the CSC Pro product page, https://sonkawadelabs.in/products/csc-pro/

**Q: Does CSC Pro guarantee my application will be approved?**
No. It is an operator assistance tool. It does not guarantee approval, issuance or success of any
government application, certificate or portal transaction. No refund is available merely because a
government portal is slow, unavailable, rejects an application, or requires correction.

## 4. Helpora Support — AI support chat for websites

**Q: What is Helpora Support?**
Helpora Support is an AI customer support assistant that a business adds to its own website. It
answers visitors' questions in plain language, day or night, using the help content the business has
given it, and hands the conversation to the business's own team when a person is needed.

**Q: How do I add it to my site?**
One line of HTML before the closing `</body>` tag. No redesign, no rebuild, and no particular
framework — it works on WordPress, Shopify, a hand-written HTML site, or anything else that lets you
add a script tag.

**Q: Where do the answers come from?**
From the help content you load into the dashboard — FAQs, help articles, policies, opening hours.
The assistant answers from that only. If something is not in your content, it says it does not know
and offers to pass the customer to your team, rather than guessing. It can only point customers to
links you have approved, so it cannot invent a URL.

**Q: What does the dashboard show me?**
Every conversation, what customers asked most, and where your help content has gaps.

**Q: What does Helpora Support cost?**
Pricing has not been announced yet. It is not available for purchase and nothing is charged for it.
Contact us and we will share pricing as soon as it is set.

**Q: How do I get an account?**
Accounts are set up by us, one at a time, during early access. Send a request from
https://sonkawadelabs.in/products/helpora/signup/ with your business name, website address, contact
name and email, where your help content lives, an escalation email address, and the languages your
customers write in. We reply within 2 business days, create your workspace, and send you a login.

**Q: How do I set it up once I have an account?**
The five-step guide is at https://sonkawadelabs.in/products/helpora/onboarding/ — find your client ID
on the Install page, load your help content, set the escalation address, add the snippet to your
site, then test it and go live.

**Q: Where do I sign in?**
https://helporasupport.sonkawadelabs.in/login

**Q: Is my content mixed with other businesses'?**
No. Each business's conversations and help content are kept separate and are never used to answer
another business's visitors.

**Q: Should customers type sensitive details into the chat?**
No. Do not enter passwords, payment card numbers or other sensitive credentials into the chat window.

## 5. StockSaathi — stock enquiries for shops

**Q: What is StockSaathi?**
StockSaathi answers a shop's customers when they ask whether an item is in stock and what it costs,
using the shop's own item and price list. Customers can ask on WhatsApp, or they can ring the shop —
if nobody picks up, the call is forwarded to the StockSaathi AI agent, which answers by voice.

**Q: Where do the prices come from?**
From the software the shop already uses for its items, stock and prices, so the answers customers get
match what is actually on the shelf.

**Q: Does the shop owner see the enquiries?**
Yes. The owner gets a summary of what customers asked for, including items that were out of stock and
worth restocking.

**Q: Is a price quoted by StockSaathi binding?**
No. An availability or price answer is information taken from the shop's records at that moment. It
is not a binding quotation, a reservation of stock, or an offer capable of acceptance. Stock may sell
out or prices may change before a customer arrives.

**Q: What does StockSaathi cost?**
Pricing has not been announced yet. It is not available for purchase and nothing is charged for it.

**Q: How does the call answering work?**
The shop sets up call forwarding on its own number with its telecom operator, so unanswered, busy or
unreachable calls reach the AI agent. Only calls the shop does not answer are forwarded — calls the
shop picks up are never processed by us. The agent tells every caller up front that it is an
automated assistant answering for the shop. Calls answered by the agent are recorded and transcribed;
a caller who does not consent may hang up and contact the shop directly. The shop's telecom operator
may charge for forwarded calls, and those charges are between the shop and the operator.

## 6. Phone Munshi — AI receptionist for tradesmen

**Q: What is Phone Munshi?**
Phone Munshi is a receptionist for plumbers, electricians, mechanics, carpenters and other tradesmen.
When a tradesman cannot answer a call, it is forwarded to the AI agent, which answers, works out what
job the caller needs, proposes a time and a price, and passes the job to the tradesman to confirm.

**Q: What happens on the call?**
The agent picks up, says it is answering on the tradesman's behalf, talks to the caller in Marathi,
Hindi or English, works out the job, and suggests a time and a price. It can follow up on WhatsApp.
The tradesman then confirms or declines with a single tap, the customer is told, and the booking is
saved.

**Q: Is the job booked automatically?**
No. No job is booked until the tradesman confirms it. A time or price the agent mentions is an
indication based on the tradesman's own configured rates — it is not a binding quotation or an
accepted contract.

**Q: Which calls get forwarded?**
Only the ones the tradesman does not answer. Calls he picks up himself are never forwarded and are
not processed by us.

**Q: Are calls recorded?**
Yes. Calls answered by the AI agent are recorded and transcribed, so the job details can be worked
out, shown to the tradesman, and checked if there is later a dispute about what was agreed. The
caller is informed at the start of the call and may end the call if they do not consent.

**Q: Who pays for call forwarding?**
The tradesman's telecom operator may charge for forwarded calls. Those charges are between the
tradesman and the operator, and are not collected by Sonkawade Labs.

**Q: What does Phone Munshi cost?**
Pricing has not been announced yet. It is not available for purchase and nothing is charged for it.

**Q: Is Phone Munshi a marketplace that finds me customers?**
No. It is not a marketplace, it does not introduce customers, and it does not perform any trade work.
It answers calls from people who were already calling the tradesman.

## 8. Refunds and cancellation

**Q: Can I get a refund on used credits?**
No. Once a credit is consumed for an application workflow it cannot be reversed, restored or redeemed
for cash.

**Q: What if I was charged twice?**
If you are charged more than once for the same top-up, the duplicate amount is refunded to the
original payment method after verification.

**Q: What if money was taken but I got nothing?**
If a payment is debited but credits or access are not delivered, and the payment gateway confirms the
debit, we will either deliver the purchased credits or refund the amount.

**Q: How long does a refund take?**
Verified refunds are initiated within 7 business days of confirmation. How long it then takes to
reach your account depends on your bank or payment provider — typically 5 to 10 business days.

**Q: How do I request a refund?**
Email sumitsonkawade@sonkawadelabs.in with the subject line "Refund Request", including your
registered email or mobile number, the payment reference / UTR / transaction ID, the payment date and
amount, and a brief description of the issue. We acknowledge refund requests within 48 hours and
resolve them within 30 days.

**Q: Can I cancel?**
Yes. You may stop using the service and cancel future renewals at any time by emailing us.
Cancellation stops future billing; fees already paid for the current billing period are
non-refundable except where required by law. Unused prepaid credits remain available until account
closure, and on closure unused credits are forfeited unless a refund is required by law or agreed in
writing.

**Q: Can I get a refund because a government portal rejected my application?**
No. No refund is available merely because a government portal is slow, unavailable, rejects an
application, or requires correction.

## 9. Privacy, data and deletion

**Q: Do you sell my data?**
No. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

**Q: Who do you share data with?**
Only where reasonably necessary: service providers that deliver the service (hosting, database,
messaging, telephony, speech and AI processing, support vendors), Cashfree Payments for payments,
regulators or courts where required by law, and a lawful successor in a merger or business transfer.

**Q: Which law applies?**
The Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Disputes are
governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Latur, Maharashtra have exclusive jurisdiction.

**Q: What are my rights over my data?**
You can request a summary of the personal data we hold about you, correction of anything inaccurate,
deletion subject to lawful retention exceptions, withdrawal of consent for future processing,
information about the categories of third parties we share with, and grievance redressal. If you are
not satisfied after our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

**Q: How do I delete my data?**
Email sumitsonkawade@sonkawadelabs.in with the subject "<Product> Data Deletion Request" — for
example "Phone Munshi Data Deletion Request". Include enough detail for us to find your record. We
acknowledge within 48 hours and respond within 30 days. Each product has its own deletion page with
the exact details to include.

**Q: Can I delete just one call recording?**
Yes. For StockSaathi and Phone Munshi, say so in your email and give the number you called from and
the approximate time of the call. You do not have to delete anything else.

**Q: Is there anything you cannot delete?**
Some records must be kept — transaction and payment records we are required to retain by law or by
our payment partner, and minimal logs kept to prevent fraud and abuse. Where we must keep something,
we tell you what and why in our response.

**Q: Who is the Grievance Officer?**
Sumit Sonkawade, Sonkawade Labs, Walandi, Deoni Taluka, Latur District, Maharashtra 413519, India. Email
sumitsonkawade@sonkawadelabs.in with the subject "Privacy Grievance". Grievances are acknowledged
within 48 hours and resolved within 30 days.

**Q: Are the policies available in Marathi or Hindi?**
The CSC Pro privacy policy and terms are published in English, Marathi and Hindi. The Helpora
Support, StockSaathi and Phone Munshi policies are published in English; a copy in Marathi, Hindi or
another language listed in the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution of India can be requested by
email.

**Q: Does the website use cookies or track me?**
The sonkawadelabs.in website does not set cookies and does not run analytics or advertising trackers.
Web fonts are loaded from a third-party CDN, which may receive your IP address as part of a normal
web request.

**Q: Is there a minimum age?**
The products are intended for users aged 18 and above. A minor should use them only with the consent
and supervision of a parent or lawful guardian.

## 10. Things the assistant must not do

- Do not quote a price for Helpora Support, StockSaathi or Phone Munshi. Their pricing is not
  published. Say so and offer to take the person's contact details.
- Do not explain how any product is built internally, what it is written in, what services it runs
  on, or how its automation works step by step. Describe what the product does for the customer.
- Do not give legal, tax, financial or medical advice, and do not interpret a government rule or say
  whether someone qualifies for a certificate or scheme.
- Do not promise a specific outcome from any government portal or authority.
- Do not confirm, guess at, or discuss the status of a specific person's account, payment,
  application or refund. Escalate to a person.
- Do not ask for or accept passwords, OTPs, card numbers, bank details, Aadhaar numbers or other
  sensitive identifiers in the chat.
- Do not invent a date, a timeline, a discount or a feature that is not written in this document.
